Civ Globe: Explore Civilizations Through Time and Place

Civ Globe is an offline civilization and heritage atlas for iPhone and iPad. Turn the globe, read about places, explore historical connections, and keep a record of your own travels.

Platforms: iPhone and iPad, iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or later.
Developer: Yi Xu.
Privacy: Civ Globe Privacy Policy.

A World to Explore

Browse cultural and natural places on an interactive globe. Find places by country, region, or category. The atlas includes World Heritage, Tentative Lists, Intangible Cultural Heritage, Memory of the World, Biosphere Reserves, Global Geoparks, and Creative Cities.

Knowledge graphs, time filters, and themed tours connect places, civilizations, and historical events. Selected places include offline architectural models or city scenes for exploring spatial structure. Civ Globe is an independent app, not an official UNESCO application.

Your Travel Passport

Record visited places, countries, and cities in a personal travel passport. With your permission, Civ Globe can read photo location and date metadata on your device to suggest travel footprints. This feature does not import or upload photo originals, or download iCloud originals for the scan.

Read Offline, Keep Records Locally

The core atlas and bundled media work offline. No account, advertising, or behavioral tracking. Personal records stay on your device, with manual backup export and import. External reference websites and map services require an internet connection.

Support and Feedback

For app issues, content corrections, or feature suggestions, email noverfitting@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, system version, steps to reproduce the issue, and what happened. A screenshot can help describe a visual issue.

Why do some entries lack photos or coordinates?

Source coverage and geographic detail vary across heritage programs. Some cultural practices do not correspond to a single precise point, and some entries have text or source links without photographs. Maps, historical illustrations, and 3D models support exploration and reading.

Can I use the app without photo or location permission?

Yes. The atlas, globe, knowledge graph, and manual travel records do not require these optional permissions. Location is used for nearby-place requests you initiate. Photo access is used for the photo-footprint feature you choose to run. You can manage permissions in system settings.

How do I back up or move personal records?

Export a Civ Globe backup from settings in the Me section, then import it in the app on another device. Keep your own backup before changing devices or addressing storage issues.
