iDevTools Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Welcome to iDevTools ("the App"). iDevTools is a lightweight web developer inspection toolkit for Safari on iPadOS and iOS. It helps users inspect page structure, network requests, cookies, storage, console output, page assets, and basic audit information directly inside Safari.

We care about privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may access, how that information is used, and how you can manage related permissions.

By using the App, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection

iDevTools is designed to provide web inspection and debugging features locally on your device.

By default, the App does not actively collect, upload, sell, or share your personally identifiable information, and it does not upload debugging data to the developer's servers.

2. Web Page Content and Developer Inspection Data

To provide inspection features, the App may access certain content and runtime information from the current web page when you actively enable and use the Safari extension or related debugging features, including but not limited to:

  • HTML structure, DOM nodes, element attributes, and inline styles
  • Scripts, stylesheets, images, and other page source assets
  • Console logs, warnings, errors, and JavaScript evaluation results
  • Network request lists, request headers, response information, timing, and waterfall data
  • Cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, and other browser storage data
  • Quick audit results such as robots.txt and sitemap checks

This information is used only to display, inspect, copy, or debug web pages within the App. Unless you actively submit information through a web page, a third-party service, or system sharing features, the App does not send this debugging data to the developer.

3. Cookies and Storage Access

The App may read cookies and storage data available to the current web page for the following purposes:

  • Displaying cookies and local storage values in the inspection panel
  • Helping users copy individual or all cookies and storage entries
  • Helping users debug login state, sessions, caching, and local configuration issues

Important notes:

  • Cookies and storage data are processed locally on your device by default
  • The App does not actively upload this data to the developer's servers
  • Copying, sharing, or pasting such data is initiated by the user; please be aware that it may contain sensitive information
  • How third-party websites handle cookies and storage data is governed by their own privacy policies

4. Console and JavaScript Execution

The App may provide a console feature that allows users to run JavaScript commands in the context of the current web page.

Commands you run may read or modify the current page state, and may trigger network requests, storage writes, or third-party script behavior controlled by the page itself. Please run commands only on websites you trust and are authorized to debug.

The App does not actively upload console input or evaluation results to the developer.

5. Network Requests and Page Audits

The App may display network request information generated by the current web page, including request URLs, request headers, response information, status codes, timing, and resource types.

When you use robots.txt, sitemap, or similar quick audit features, the App may send necessary requests to the current website or a website you specify in order to fetch the relevant files and display the audit results. These requests are sent to the target website, not to the developer's servers.

6. Local Data Storage

To improve the user experience, the App may store a small amount of settings or state information locally on your device, including but not limited to:

  • Panel position, size, and layout preferences
  • Light/dark mode or interface preferences
  • User-defined debugging settings
  • Temporary debugging state or recent usage records

These data are stored locally on your device by default. You may clear related local data through system settings, in-app settings, or by uninstalling the App.

7. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

By default, the App does not sell, rent, share, or transfer users' personal data to third parties.

When you inspect third-party web pages, those pages may load third-party scripts, ads, analytics, or other services. Such behavior is controlled by the websites you visit and their third-party providers, and is not data collection performed by the App.

If the App integrates crash analytics, usage analytics, or other third-party services in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly and will describe the relevant data processing practices.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for developers, designers, and technical users. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy based on feature changes, platform requirements, or legal and regulatory requirements.

The updated version will be published in the App or on the relevant page.
