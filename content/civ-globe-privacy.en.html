Civ Globe Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. Developer: Yi Xu.

Civ Globe is a civilization, heritage, and travel-footprint atlas for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains local records and optional permissions.

1. Accounts and Data Collection

Civ Globe does not require an account or integrate advertising or behavioral tracking. It does not upload atlas browsing history, travel footprints, notes, photographs, or your current location to developer-operated servers. The core atlas and bundled media are included in the app and work offline.

2. Personal Records on Your Device

Saved places, visits, travel footprints, notes, recent browsing, and language preferences are stored on your device. The app does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization. You can manually export and import backups. System device backups, sharing tools, or third-party storage services you choose may process backup data under their own policies.

3. Optional Location Permission

With system permission, current location is used to calculate distances when you ask to find nearby places. The app does not continuously track your route or upload your current location to the developer. Travel footprints that you manually save or confirm from photo matches remain as personal records on your device.

4. Optional Photo Permission

When you choose to discover footprints from photos and grant access, the app reads permitted photos' location, date, and local identifier metadata to match places and cities on your device. Limited photo access processes only photos you allow.

This feature does not import or upload photo originals, or download iCloud originals for the scan. Match results and travel records that you confirm are stored on your device.

5. Device Motion and External Services

Some travel-mark visual effects use device orientation to adjust reflections. Motion data is not recorded or uploaded.

When you choose to open a source website, Wikipedia, or an external map, that service may receive network requests and necessary destination parameters. Its own privacy policy applies.

6. Managing Permissions and Records

You can manage photo and location permissions in system settings, edit personal records in the app, and manually export backups. The app has no account on developer-operated servers to close.

7. Contacting the Developer

If you voluntarily email support, the developer receives the contact details and issue information you send and uses them to respond. Your email provider handles email under its own policy. This does not change the app's default on-device processing of personal records.

8. Updates and Contact

If data-handling practices change with app features, this policy will be updated and the date above will be revised.

For privacy questions or technical support, email noverfitting@gmail.com. Visit Civ Globe Support.
